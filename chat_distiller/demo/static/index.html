What the guardrail does

  1. 01
    Validate

    Stable schema, topic grammar, byte limits. Corruption refuses service.

  2. 02
    Surface overlap

    Lexical overlap above a threshold is a suspicion, never a verdict.

  3. 03
    Require a decision

    Supersede the old claim, mark the new one disputed, or cancel. No silent wins.

Conflict matching is deterministic and lexical. It can miss semantic contradictions — and it never adjudicates truth.

      Data safety
      
          
      Backups you can verify. Removal you can audit.
      
          
      Every snapshot carries a checksummed manifest. Redaction keeps a content-free tombstone and the supersession chain; deletion removes rows and names what references them. Both write an audit entry and a plaintext pre-change backup by default.
      
        
      
      
      

      
      
        
      
          
      
            
      Snapshots
      
            
          
      
          
      Snapshots are written next to the store with a manifest: claim count, byte count and two SHA-256 digests.
      
          
      
        
      

        
      
          
      
            
      Audit trail
      
            –
          
      
          
      Append-only. Records the action, claim ids, before/after store digests and the backup filename — never the claim text.
      
          
      
        
      
      
      

      
      
        
      What this guarantees — and what it does not
      
        
        
          
      • Writes are serialized by a cross-process lock; contention past the timeout is an explicit busy error, never a silent overwrite.
        • 
          
      • Restore verifies byte count, file digest, store digest and claim count against the manifest before touching the store.
        • 
          
      • Backups are plaintext and local — protect the backups directory like the store itself.
        • 
          
      • Redaction does not reach transcripts, logs, old backups or derived evidence indexes; rebuild or purge those separately.
        • 
        
      
        
      CLI equivalents: claim backup · claim backups · claim audit · claim restore --from … · claim redact … --yes · claim delete … --yes
